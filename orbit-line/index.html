You fly the slowest shuttle in the system. There's no friction out here, so the shuttle keeps drifting while you're in the back dealing with the passengers.

Your three jobs

  • Enforce today's rulesPress Tab to turn round and face the cabin. Click a passenger who's breaking a rule to slap them. Two strikes and they leave by the airlock at the next dock. Slap an innocent one and the whole cabin turns on you.
  • Dock at every stationDrift into the dashed docking ring slowly enough and the clamps catch you. Shipping lanes are optional. The asteroid belt is a shortcut, and so is the planet's gravity.
  • Beat the clockHappy passengers say thank you as they leave. Each thank-you adds six seconds.

Controls

W main thruster
S reverse thruster
AD rotate
Space stabilisers (slow down)
Tab or E turn to the cabin
Click or F slap (in the cabin)
Hold M map + rules
H horn · R tow back to a lane
Shift 1 · 0 thank-yous banked
