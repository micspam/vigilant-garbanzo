A playable greybox built from one store page and ten screenshots. You drive the town bus. Keep the riders in line, make your stops, and beat the clock.

Your three jobs

  • Enforce today's rulesThey're on your map. Slap anyone breaking one. Two strikes and they're off at the next stop. Slap someone innocent and the whole bus turns on you.
  • Make your stopsThe dash sign names the next one. Pull up by the ringed marker and stop. Roads are a suggestion; the cornfield is a shortcut.
  • Beat the clockHappy riders say thank you as they get off. Each thank-you adds six seconds.

Controls

WS drive / reverse
AD steer
Space brake
Mouse look around
Click or F slap
Hold M map + rules
QE turn head · C centre
H horn · R unstick bus
